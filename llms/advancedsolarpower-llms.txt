# Advanced Solar Power (Hangzhou) Inc.

> Chinese photovoltaic manufacturer founded in 2008, producing cadmium telluride (CdTe) thin-film
> solar modules, PV system engineering, and building-integrated photovoltaic (BIPV) products.
> The first CdTe thin-film module manufacturer in China. Sells physical hardware and system
> integration only — there is no public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable interface.
